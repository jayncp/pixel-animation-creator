# Method provenance and limits

This skill distills observed failures and fixes in an iterative reference-to-pixel-animation study: identity drift across views, unnecessary detail loss attributed to resolution, mechanical same-phase motion, hard source-view switches, root-connected but jumping tails, source fragments mistaken for anatomy, far sleeves exposed by longer holds, noisy edges, and palette/timing errors in otherwise plausible previews.

These are transferable diagnosis patterns, not a demand for the study's character, number of appendages, 256-pixel canvas, four action names, or frame counts. A detailed working canvas was useful in that study but did not prove a universal minimum. Hybrid procedural/native results improved through critique but retained limits; no one-call natural-animation guarantee was established.

The study inspected actual tutorial GIF timing and found both repeated equal-duration poses forming longer holds and equal-duration frames producing acceleration through spacing. Do not copy tutorial timing or a whole multi-action GIF's duration as a required recipe. No third-party tutorial images or GIFs are bundled here. The companion archive contains the project's own more detailed research and inspection evidence.
